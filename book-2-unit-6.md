# Numbers from 100 to 1 million in Spanish

For hundreds:

    100 - cien

    101 to 199 - ciento

    200 to 299 - doscientos

    300 to 399 - trescientos

    400 to 499 - cuatrocientos

    500 to 599 - quinientos[1]

    600 to 699 - seiscientos

    700 to 799 - setecientos[1]

    800 to 899 - ochocientos

    900 to 999 - novecientos[1]

[1] Irregular: it doesn't follow the number + *cientos* pattern.

For thousands:

    1.000 - mil

    2.000 - dos mil

    3.000 - tres mil

    etc.

For millions:

    1.000.000 - un millón

    2.000.000 - dos millones

    etc.

In most Spanish-speaking countries we use periods instead of commas for thousands.

Hundreds from 200 through 900 have gender in Spanish. When they go before a noun, they match its gender:
*cuatrocientos dieciséis gatos, quinientas quince sillas.*

We don't add any extra *y* when using hundreds. For example, 457 would be *cuatrocientos cincuenta y siete;*
it would never be *cuatrocientos "y" cincuenta y siete.*
