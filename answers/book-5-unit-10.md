# Answer key — Book 5, Unit 10

The unit 10 homework is a memorization / review exercise. For each verb the conjugations are listed in the order: yo, tú, él/ella/usted, nosotros, (vosotros), ellos/ellas/ustedes.

The "imperative tú" line shows the affirmative and negative forms (e.g. *canta | no cantes*).
